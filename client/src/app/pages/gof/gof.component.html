01 - Introduction

What are Design Patterns?

Design patterns are reusable solutions to commonly occurring problems in software design. They are not finished code — they are templates describing how to solve a problem that can be used in many different situations.


They were formalised in 1994 by Erich Gamma, Richard Helm, Ralph Johnson, and John Vlissides — known as the Gang of Four — in their book Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software.

"Program to an interface, not an implementation." - Gang of Four, Design Patterns (1994)

Patterns give developers a shared vocabulary. Saying "use a Factory here" communicates a full architectural idea instantly. They also encode hard-won wisdom about what works — and what breaks — at scale.
